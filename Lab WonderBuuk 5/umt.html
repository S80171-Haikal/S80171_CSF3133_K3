About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as a Fisheries and Marine Science Centre of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in 1979 before being established as an independent university college in 2001 and upgraded to a full-fledged public university in 2007.

UMT is widely recognized as Malaysia's premier university focusing on marine science, fisheries, and aquatic resources. Situated along the coast facing the South China Sea, its campus provides direct access to rich marine ecosystems for hands-on research and environmental conservation.

In addition to marine and environmental studies, the university offers a wide range of academic programmes in computer science, maritime management, business, and applied sciences, preparing students to contribute to sustainable development globally.
